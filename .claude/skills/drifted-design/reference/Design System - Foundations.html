Drifted Marketing · Design system · Foundations v1

Tokens

Everything in this document is a named token with a fixed value. Locked inputs: the D mark on the 1A skeleton with misregistration; Schibsted Grotesk 900 wordmark, never offset; Blunt Force territory, refined and engineered; Tar base, Voltage hero, Siren support. The Tailwind config at the end of the colour section is the machine-readable version of the whole thing.

01

Colour

Four families. Tar and Newsprint carry every surface; Voltage is the hero and appears on every screen; Siren is support and appears once or twice. Values are engineered off default fluorescent — the lime is greened so it reads anodized, the red has the pink removed.

Schibsted Grotesk for display and every lockup — a neo-grotesque whose stem is 0.30 of cap, matching the mark exactly. Instrument Sans for body. JetBrains Mono for labels, metadata and data. Three registers, nothing in between.

Signature move 01 — cap-tight knockout
Look bigger
than you are

A Voltage block clipped to cap height, not the line box — line-height:.78; padding:.1em .06em .04em. It sits a hair below the baseline like a printed knockout and never bleeds into the line beneath. One highlighted word per headline, always a noun or a verb, never an adjective. Token: .drift-fill.

Signature move 02 — the locked stack
We driftedfrom howagencies work

Alternating solid and 1.5px outlined lines inside one headline block, set at leading 0.84 so the lines nearly touch. The outline is always the middle line of three, or the second of two. It keeps a wall of 900-weight type readable at billboard size and is the only place a text stroke is permitted. Token: -webkit-text-stroke: 1.5px var(--text-primary).

Full-bleed rules. Marquees, accent bands, image fields and the closing CTA break the 1440 cap and run edge to edge. Everything else stays inside it. Text never sits in a full-bleed band without its own inner container at the same margins. Body copy is capped at 62ch regardless of column span. Only the nav is fixed; nothing else in the system is position:fixed.

04

Misregistration as a system

The offset is a brand device, not a logo detail. Anywhere it appears it uses the same two numbers — no per-component values, no eyeballing.

Tokens
--misreg-x−9px
--misreg-y−8px
--misreg-rotate−3.5deg
--misreg-tint0.34

At sizes above 200px the offset scales with the element: 9 units = 9% of element width. Below 64px it stays fixed at 9px so it never disappears.

Where it applies
The markAlways, in every two-layer reproduction.
Image treatmentsA Voltage or Siren plate offset behind a photo or video frame.
Card hoverAn accent plate slides out from behind the card on hover — 160ms, and it returns on leave.
Section dividersA double rule, the lower one offset and 34% tint.
Stat calloutsThe figure duplicated behind itself in Voltage at 34%.
404 and stingsFree rein — the device can be exaggerated to 18–24 units.
Where it never applies
The wordmarkDRIFTED is always set square and single-layer.
Body copyAny text below 24px. Legibility outranks the device.
UI controlsButtons, inputs, toggles, tabs — anything a user clicks or types into.
Functional dataTables, dashboards, invoices, pricing.
Anything movingNever animate the offset on scroll; it is a resting state, not a parallax effect.

Named transitions. hard-cut-wipe — accent plate wipes across on clip-path, 280ms ease-cut, foreground flips colour at the midpoint; replaces every fade in the system. slam — content lands from 1.06 scale and 14px with zero overshoot in 120ms, then a 6px Siren bar shoots underneath over 160ms. mark-drift — the logo's 1,150ms separation. Never bounce, spring, elastic, or anything over 520ms outside the mark.
