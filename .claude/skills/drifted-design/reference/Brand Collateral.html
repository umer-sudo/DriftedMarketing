Social rules
One accent per post Tar + Voltage, or Newsprint + Siren. Never Voltage and Siren in the same frame. · Mark Always bottom-left, 64px on a 1080 canvas, two-layer; on a Voltage field it flips to Tar-on-Tar at 30% tint. · Type Display 900 only; body copy never appears on a post — if it needs a paragraph it is a carousel. · Safe area 80px on square, 150px top and bottom on stories. · Misregistration Mark only, plus one optional accent moment per grid of nine.

Document rules

The seven decisions
01 Tar #0A0A0B is the base. 02 Voltage #AFE304 is the hero and appears on everything. 03 Siren #F5330A is support and appears once. 04 Schibsted Grotesk 900, uppercase, tracked −4.5%, is the only display voice. 05 Instrument Sans body, JetBrains Mono labels at +0.18em. 06 The mark is two plates 9 units apart at −3.5°; the wordmark never is. 07 Grain on every flat field — 8% on dark, 26% on accent. Everything on this page is those seven decisions applied to a different rectangle.
